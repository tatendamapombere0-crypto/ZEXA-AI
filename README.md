# ZEXA AI - ZIMSEC Revision & Offline AI Tutor

Zimbabwe's intelligent ZIMSEC curriculum study assistant, past examination paper bank, and offline AI revision tutor for Primary (Grade 7), Secondary (O-Level & A-Level), and Tertiary students.

## Features

- **ZEXA AI Interactive Chat**:
  - Offline-first intelligent AI tutor aligned with the Zimbabwe School Examinations Council (ZIMSEC) syllabus.
  - Syllabus-grounded retrieval-augmented responses (RAG) with source topic citations.
  - Realistic token streaming with prefill and generation metrics (`Prefill: 0.1s | Total: 1.2s • Syllabus Match`).
  - Daily query tracker (2 free questions per day for free tier, reset daily; unlimited for Premium & Holiday Free users).
- **ZIMSEC Past Exam Paper Bank (Exam Mode)**:
  - Over 90 authentic ZIMSEC past papers and practice banks covering 27 subjects across Grade 7, O-Level, and A-Level.
  - Interactive multiple-choice tests with real-time feedback, detailed marking explanations, score tracking, and ZIMSEC grade calculation (A to U).
  - One-click "Ask ZEXA to explain this question" integration.
- **ZIMSEC Study Pack & Syllabus Explorer**:
  - Searchable curriculum modules across Mathematics, Combined Science, Biology, Chemistry, Physics, Geography, History, Agriculture, Shona, Ndebele, Computer Science, and more.
  - Direct "Ask ZEXA about this topic" action.
- **Academic Term & Subscription Management**:
  - Full Zimbabwe school academic term calendar (Term 1, Term 2, Term 3, and inter-term holiday breaks).
  - Offline daily activation code validation (`Day|Year|Term|Month|SecretKey`).
  - EcoCash USSD payment simulation and instant activation.

## Tech Stack

- React 19 + TypeScript + Vite
- Tailwind CSS
